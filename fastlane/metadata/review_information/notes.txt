PDFLito needs no account and makes no network connections. To test: tap "Scannen", scan any printed page. Each page is checked for sharpness on device; blurry pages are marked "Unscharf" and must be rescanned. The file name is suggested from the largest heading. Saving to a cloud folder (e.g. Google Drive or iCloud Drive) works via Settings > "Ordner wählen", which uses the standard Files picker.
